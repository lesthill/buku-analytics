BUKU Analytics — Privacy Policy

BUKU Analytics reads accounting data from BUKU Shipping, Inc.'s own QuickBooks Online company, using access granted by a BUKU QuickBooks administrator.

  • What it reads: BUKU's accounting records and reports (for example customers, vendors, invoices, payments and financial reports).
  • How it is used: internal BUKU reporting and analysis only.
  • Sharing: none. The data is not sold or shared outside BUKU.
  • Security: access tokens are kept in BUKU's password vault and data is transmitted over encrypted connections.
  • Disconnecting: a BUKU QuickBooks administrator can disconnect the app at any time in QuickBooks under Apps; access stops immediately.
